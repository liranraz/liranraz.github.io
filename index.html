🧭 Orienteering

Orienteering is a sport where participants navigate between control points in diverse terrain using only a detailed map and compass. It combines physical endurance with sharp navigation skills — you run through forests, across hills, and along trails, making split-second route choices under pressure. Courses range from beginner-friendly park trails to advanced wilderness challenges spanning several kilometers of off-trail forest.

I'm an active member of the Ottawa Orienteering Club (OOC), where I participate regularly in meets and also serve as a Meet Director and Course Controller — responsible for designing courses, placing controls in the terrain, verifying course quality, and managing event logistics. I've designed courses across Gatineau Park, Stony Swamp, Carman Trails, and other local venues in the Ottawa–Gatineau region.

About Me

Originally from Israel, I hold a B.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering (Cum Laude) from the Technion — Israel Institute of Technology. After graduating, I transitioned into embedded software development and moved to Canada, where I've spent 17+ years building radio and telecom systems at Nortel Networks and Ericsson in Ottawa.

My day-to-day work involves low-level C/C++ programming on embedded platforms — writing device drivers, firmware, and radio control software for LTE and 5G base stations. I enjoy the challenge of working close to the hardware, debugging complex timing issues, and making systems reliable.

Outside of work, I'm a self-taught Android developer with 13 apps published on Google Play, mostly educational games focused on Hebrew language learning and word puzzles. I also serve as a Meet Director and Course Controller for the Ottawa Orienteering Club, designing navigation courses across Gatineau Park and the Ottawa region.
